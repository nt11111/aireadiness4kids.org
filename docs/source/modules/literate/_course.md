# AI Literate (6-8)

**Formerly:** AI Investigators. **Writer:** Rishi. **Peer checker:** Neil. **Theme:** Critical Thinking & Ethics.[ Slides folder](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1yp7PwpzLostPpjQt3sKvzkiMISs6vFF4)

**Course description (the site's text, with the new course name):** The AI Literate course goes deeper: how does AI actually work, what are its real-world consequences, and what does responsible use look like in school and daily life?

**What learners get from it (on the site now):**

- How machine learning uses training data to find patterns and make predictions
- How bias gets into AI systems, and who it affects most
- Where helpful AI use ends and academic dishonesty begins
- How to spot AI-generated media and fact-check what you see online
- What data AI apps collect, and practical ways to protect yours
- How to reason through real AI ethics dilemmas with a clear framework

**Voice:** Ages 11 to 13. Up to 250 words per step and 18 words per sentence. Direct and curious. Examples from school, group chats, apps, and games.
