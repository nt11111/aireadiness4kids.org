### 1. A recent case for Step 1 or Step 2

In 2024, University of Washington researchers gave AI models the same resumes with different names attached. The models favored names linked to white people 85% of the time. They favored names linked to women 11% of the time.

[CALLOUT think] The resumes were the same. So where did the difference come from?

Source: AI bias in resume screening (Wilson and Caliskan, University of Washington, October 31, 2024) ->[https://www.washington.edu/news/2024/10/31/ai-bias-resume-screening-race-gender/](https://www.washington.edu/news/2024/10/31/ai-bias-resume-screening-race-gender/)

### 2. ARK's view (new in guide 1.4)

**ARK's view:** We think removing names and photos isn't enough to make a tool fair. Someone has to test the results for every group, before and after launch.

**The best case against:** Testing every group costs time and money. Small companies may skip AI tools entirely, or hide that they use them.
