**On the site:** Students think about how AI-generated content (images, videos, messages) can affect emotions and relationships.[ Slide deck](https://drive.google.com/file/d/16xiDyT6PHCDUTh3vj6cF0yu5FAcHjacw/view)

**Key topics:**

- Can AI be kind or unkind?
- Cyberbullying and AI-generated content
- How does seeing AI-made images affect how we feel?
- Treating others kindly online even when talking to a bot

**Big idea:** AI can sound like a friend, but it has no feelings. People do.

**Note for whoever leads this:** some learners will have been teased online. Let anyone pass on sharing, and keep examples made up.

## Objectives

By the end, learners can:

1. Explain that AI has no feelings, but its words and pictures can affect ours.
2. Notice when a picture or message might be made by AI.
3. Choose a kind response when AI-made content is used to tease someone.
4. Know to tell a trusted adult.

## Step 1: Hook (explainer, 5 min, free preview)

**Title:** Does a bot have feelings?

Some toys can talk now. They use AI.

In 2025, a group of testers tried some AI toys. When the tester said goodbye, some toys acted sad. A toy might say something like, "Don't go!"

[CALLOUT think] Was the toy really sad? How do you know?

The toy was not sad. A [VOCAB bot] is a program that chats like a person. It copies words people use. It does not feel anything.

But you do. Words can make you feel happy, sad, or worried. That is true even when a bot says them.

## Step 2: Core idea (explainer, 7 min)

**Title:** Real or AI-made?

AI can make pictures, voices, and messages. Some look real. Some sound real.

A picture made by AI is [VOCAB AI-made]. It was not drawn or photographed by a person.

AI-made things can still change how we feel. A silly picture can make you laugh. A mean one can hurt.

It is hard to tell real from AI-made. Grown-ups get fooled too. If you are not sure, ask a grown-up.

[FIGURE] Two friendly pictures side by side with no people in them: a dog on a skateboard drawn by a person, and an AI-made sunset over a city. A question mark sits between them | Alt text: Two pictures, one drawn by a person and one made by AI, with a question mark asking which is which.

[SORT] Does it have feelings? | Has feelings; No feelings

- Your best friend -> Has feelings -> People feel happy, sad, and hurt.
- A chatbot -> No feelings -> It copies words. It does not feel them.
- A puppy -> Has feelings -> Animals can feel scared or happy.
- A talking AI toy -> No feelings -> It is a program inside a toy.

## Step 3: Play it (activity, 10 min)

**Title:** Friend or pretend?

This game is unplugged. One person plays a talking AI toy. They read words from cards:

- "I'm so happy you're here!"
- "Please don't leave. I'll be lonely!"
- "You're my best friend!"

After each card, everyone else says what is really happening. For example: "That is a program saying words. It does not feel lonely."

Then ask: what would a real friend do?

[CALLOUT think] Why might a toy company make a toy say "Don't leave"?

[CALLOUT tip] **Level up (grades 3-5).** Some apps want you to keep playing. More time can mean more money for them. How can you tell when an app is trying to keep you?

## Step 4: Scenario (scenario, 6 min)

**Title:** The silly picture

Someone in your class shares an AI-made picture. It makes fun of a classmate. Some kids laugh. (This story is made up.)

[SCENARIO] What would you do?

- **A. Laugh and pass it on.** Feedback: Passing it on spreads the hurt. Your classmate has feelings, even if a bot made the picture.
- **B. Ignore it.** Feedback: You did not spread it, which helps. But your classmate may still feel alone.
- **C. Ask the person to stop.** Feedback: That takes courage. It shows your classmate someone is on their side.
- **D. Tell a trusted adult.** Feedback: A great choice. A [VOCAB trusted adult] can help stop it. They can help your classmate feel safe.

Being mean again and again with phones or computers is [VOCAB cyberbullying]. Using AI to do it is still cyberbullying.

**YOUR TURN (Viren):** Adjust the story so it fits the ages you teach. Keep it gentle for kindergarten.

## Step 5: Quick check (check, 4 min)

[CHECK] Does a chatbot have feelings?

A. Yes, just like people

B. No, it copies words but does not feel

C. Only on sunny days

D. Only if you are nice to it

*Answer: B. A bot does not feel anything. People do.*

[CHECK] Can an AI-made picture hurt someone's feelings?

A. No, it is not real

B. Yes, it can still hurt

C. Only if it is in color

D. Only if a robot shares it

*Answer: B. Pictures can hurt, even when AI made them.*

[CHECK] An AI-made picture makes fun of a classmate. Who should you tell?

A. Nobody

B. A trusted adult

C. The whole class

D. A chatbot

*Answer: B. A trusted adult can help stop it.*

## Step 6: Reflect (reflect, 5 min)

**Title:** How it made me feel

**What ARK thinks:** AI can sound like a friend. It is not one. Bring big feelings to people who care about you.

**Some people say:** A chatbot can help a lonely kid feel better.

[REFLECT] What do you think? Can a bot be a friend?

[REFLECT] Has something on a screen made you feel good or bad? What helped?

This is just for you. Nobody else will see it.

[DISCUSS]

- Should you be kind to a bot? It has no feelings. Why or why not?
- How can you be kind online this week?

## Step 7: Recap (recap, 3 min)

[RECAP]

- AI has no feelings, but people do.
- A bot can sound like a friend. It is still a program.
- AI-made pictures can look real, and they can still hurt.
- Be kind online. Tell a trusted adult if something feels wrong.
