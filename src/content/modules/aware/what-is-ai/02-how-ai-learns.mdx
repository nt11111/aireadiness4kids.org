AI does not know things the way you do. It learns from examples.

Say we want an AI to spot cats. People show it thousands of cat photos. The AI looks for a <Vocab term="pattern" />. Pointy ears. Whiskers. A long tail.

Later it sees a new photo. It checks for those patterns. Then it guesses: cat or not a cat.

All those examples have a name. They are called <Vocab term="training data" />.

Not every clever machine is AI. A calculator follows rules. It does not learn. A <Vocab term="robot" /> is a machine that moves. Some robots use AI. Many do not.
