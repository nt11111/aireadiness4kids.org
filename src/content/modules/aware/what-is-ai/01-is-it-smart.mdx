A phone can finish your sentence. A TV app can pick a show you might like. A speaker can answer a question out loud.

How do they do that? Nobody is hiding inside. These tools use something called <Vocab term="artificial intelligence (AI)">artificial intelligence</Vocab>. We say AI for short.

AI is a computer program that learns from examples. Then it makes a guess.

<Callout kind="think">
Has a phone or tablet ever guessed what you wanted? What did it guess?
</Callout>

In this lesson you will find out what AI is. You will also learn what it is not.
