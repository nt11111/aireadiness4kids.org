## Timing for a 45-60 minute session

| Minutes | What happens | Format |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0-3 | Pre-check, read aloud | Whole group |
| 3-8 | Step 1: "Has a device ever guessed?" | Whole group |
| 8-18 | Step 2 and the sort as a stand-up game | Whole group |
| 18-28 | Step 3 Play it: You are the AI | Small groups |
| 28-35 | Step 4 scenario vote | Whole group |
| 35-40 | Step 5 quick check | Whole group |
| 40-47 | Step 6: What ARK thinks, then reflect | Individual, then pairs |
| 47-52 | Step 7 recap and post-check | Whole group |

## Before you start

- Every activity is unplugged or led by an adult. Learners do not use AI tools themselves.
- For grades K-2, skip the Level up box and act out Step 3 with real fruit if you can.
- For grades 3-5, use the Level up box and ask learners to invent their own "tricky card."
- "What ARK thinks" is an opinion. Say so out loud, and let kids disagree.

## Discussion prompts

Use the two in Step 6.

## Answer notes

Quick check: 1 B, 2 C, 3 B, 4 B. Pre/post: 1 C, 2 B, 3 A. In Step 3, the strawberry and tomato are good tricky cards: red, round, and not apples.
