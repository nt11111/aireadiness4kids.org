---
title: "You are the AI"
type: activity
minutes: 10
---

This game is unplugged. Your grown-up shows six picture cards. Each one says "apple" or "not an apple." Every apple card shows a red apple.

Now you are the AI. Look for the pattern. What makes an apple an apple?

Your grown-up holds up a new card. It is a green apple.

<Callout kind="think">
Is it an apple? What would your AI say?
</Callout>

Your AI might say "not an apple." It only saw red apples. That is not silly. It did what its examples taught it.

Now fix your AI. Which new cards would you add to the pile?

<Callout kind="tip" title="Level up (grades 3-5)" optional>
Say every dog photo an AI saw had grass in it. Then it sees a cat on grass. What might it guess? What did it really learn?
</Callout>
