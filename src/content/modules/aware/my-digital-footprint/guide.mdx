## Timing for a 45-60 minute session

| Minutes | What happens | Format |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0-3 | Pre-check, read aloud | Whole group |
| 3-8 | Step 1: footprints in the sand | Whole group |
| 8-18 | Step 2 and the share-or-private sort | Small groups |
| 18-28 | Step 3 Play it: The footprint trail | Small groups |
| 28-35 | Step 4: the friendly chatbot | Whole group |
| 35-39 | Step 5 quick check | Whole group |
| 39-47 | Step 6: What ARK thinks, then reflect | Individual, then pairs |
| 47-52 | Step 7 recap, post-check, and certificate | Whole group |

## Before you start

- Use only made-up facts on the footprints. Never ask learners for real information about themselves.
- Learners do not use AI tools or AI toys in this session.
- The school-name card can spark debate. Some families share it freely. Talk about who can see it.
- "What ARK thinks" is an opinion. Say so out loud, and let kids disagree.

## Discussion prompts

Use the two in Step 6.

## Answer notes

Quick check: 1 B, 2 B, 3 B. Pre/post: 1 A, 2 B, 3 B. In the sort, accept "Ask a grown-up" for the school name, too.
