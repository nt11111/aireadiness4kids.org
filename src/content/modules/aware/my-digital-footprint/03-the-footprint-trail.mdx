This game is unplugged. Each player gets five paper footprints. On each one, a grown-up writes a small made-up fact. For example: "likes soccer" or "has a dog named Max." Or: "walks past the big library."

Lay the footprints in a trail. Then a "detective" picks up the trail.

Can the detective guess things about the made-up kid? Where they live? What their password might be?

<Callout kind="think">
One footprint told the detective very little. What did all five tell them together?
</Callout>

Small facts add up. That is why you share the least you can.

<Callout kind="tip" title="Level up (grades 3-5)" optional>
A US law called COPPA protects kids under 13. Many apps must ask a parent before collecting a child's information. The rules got stronger in 2025.
</Callout>
