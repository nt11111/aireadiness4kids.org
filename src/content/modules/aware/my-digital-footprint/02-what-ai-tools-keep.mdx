What you type or say to an app can be saved. It can be used later.

Some AI toys record kids' voices. Many chatbots learn from what people type to them.

Some facts are <Vocab term="personal information" />. Your full name, your address, your school, and your birthday are personal. Keep them <Vocab term="private" />. Your <Vocab term="password" /> is always private.
