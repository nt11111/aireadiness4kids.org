A fuzzy prompt gets a fuzzy answer. A clear prompt gets a better one.

Fuzzy: "Tell me about dogs."

Clear: "Tell me three fun facts about puppies for a first grader."

The clear prompt says three things:

- **Who** it is for: a first grader.
- **What** you want: fun facts about puppies.
- **How much:** three facts.

<Sort />

Even a clear prompt can get a wrong answer. So check it with a second <Vocab term="source" />. A book or a teacher works.
