title: "Prompt and check"
type: activity
minutes: 10
sources:
  - title: "High school students practicing math with GPT-4 (Bastani et al., PNAS, 2025)"
    url: "https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40560616/"
---

This game is unplugged or led by your grown-up. Learners do not type into an AI tool.

Your grown-up is the AI. Give them a fuzzy prompt, like "Draw an animal." Your grown-up draws anything at all.

Now try: "Draw a happy cat in a red hat." Is the drawing closer?

Next, your grown-up gives an "AI answer" to a question. Find a book that checks it. Was the answer right?

<Callout kind="think">
Which worked better, the fuzzy prompt or the clear one? Why?
</Callout>

<Callout kind="tip" title="Level up (grades 3-5)" optional>
Older students practiced math with a chatbot. When it was taken away, they did worse on a test. Students whose chatbot gave hints mostly did not. Why might hints help more?
</Callout>
