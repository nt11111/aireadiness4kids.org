Some toys can talk now. They use AI.

In 2025, a group of testers tried some AI toys. When the tester said goodbye, some toys acted sad. A toy might say something like, "Don't go!"

<Callout kind="think">
Was the toy really sad? How do you know?
</Callout>

The toy was not sad. A <Vocab term="bot" /> is a program that chats like a person. It copies words people use. It does not feel anything.

But you do. Words can make you feel happy, sad, or worried. That is true even when a bot says them.
