## Timing for a 45-60 minute session

| Minutes | What happens | Format |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0-3 | Pre-check, read aloud | Whole group |
| 3-9 | Step 1: the AI toy that "acts sad" | Whole group |
| 9-19 | Step 2: real or AI-made, then the feelings sort | Whole group |
| 19-29 | Step 3 Play it: Friend or pretend? | Small groups |
| 29-36 | Step 4: the silly picture | Whole group |
| 36-40 | Step 5 quick check | Whole group |
| 40-47 | Step 6: What ARK thinks, then reflect | Individual, then pairs |
| 47-52 | Step 7 recap and post-check | Whole group |

## Before you start

- Some learners have been teased online. Never ask who. Let anyone pass.
- Keep examples made up. Don't show real AI-made pictures of people.
- Learners do not use AI tools or AI toys in this session.
- If a learner shares something that worries you, follow your school's safeguarding steps.
- "What ARK thinks" is an opinion. Say so out loud, and let kids disagree.

## Discussion prompts

Use the two in Step 6.

## Answer notes

Quick check: 1 B, 2 B, 3 B. Pre/post: 1 B, 2 B, 3 A. In Step 4, C and D are both strong. A good answer can be "both."
