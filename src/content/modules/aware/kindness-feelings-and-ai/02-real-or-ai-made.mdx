AI can make pictures, voices, and messages. Some look real. Some sound real.

A picture made by AI is <Vocab term="AI-made" />. It was not drawn or photographed by a person.

AI-made things can still change how we feel. A silly picture can make you laugh. A mean one can hurt.

It is hard to tell real from AI-made. Grown-ups get fooled too. If you are not sure, ask a grown-up.
