---
title: "Friend or pretend?"
type: activity
minutes: 10
---

This game is unplugged. One person plays a talking AI toy. They read words from cards:

- "I'm so happy you're here!"
- "Please don't leave. I'll be lonely!"
- "You're my best friend!"

After each card, everyone else says what is really happening. For example: "That is a program saying words. It does not feel lonely."

Then ask: what would a real friend do?

<Callout kind="think">
Why might a toy company make a toy say "Don't leave"?
</Callout>

<Callout kind="tip" title="Level up (grades 3-5)" optional>
Some apps want you to keep playing. More time can mean more money for them. How can you tell when an app is trying to keep you?
</Callout>
