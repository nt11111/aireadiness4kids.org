AI learns from examples. So its mistakes often come from its examples.

**Reason 1: too few examples.** An AI that saw only red apples may miss a green one.

**Reason 2: wrong examples.** If the examples were wrong, the AI learns wrong things. Some examples leave people out. Then the AI may not be <Vocab term="fair" /> to them.

Here is a tricky part. AI can sound very sure, even when it is wrong. Sounding sure is not proof.

So when should you <Vocab term="double-check" />? It depends on what happens if the answer is wrong.
