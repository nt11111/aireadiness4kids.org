---
title: "Sure, but wrong"
type: activity
minutes: 10
---

This game is unplugged. Your grown-up reads four facts. They use a very sure robot voice. Some are true. Some are not.

- "Spiders have eight legs."
- "The sun is a planet."
- "Penguins live at the North Pole."
- "An octopus has three hearts."

Vote with your thumb: up for true, down for false.

Now check each one in a book or with your grown-up.

<Callout kind="fact">
Spiders have eight legs. An octopus has three hearts. The sun is a star, not a planet. Most penguins live in the south, far from the North Pole.
</Callout>

<Callout kind="think">
Did the sure voice make the wrong facts feel true?
</Callout>

<Callout kind="tip" title="Level up (grades 3-5)" optional>
Write your own "sure but wrong" fact. Can your partner catch it with a book?
</Callout>
