You open Instagram and the first Reel is exactly the kind of video you'd save. Your Explore page is somehow full of stuff you'd screenshot and send to a friend. It can feel like the app is reading your mind.

It isn't. It's making a <Vocab term="prediction" />, a best guess about what you'll like. Predictions come from patterns.

Most of the AI you use every day was built with <Vocab term="machine learning" />. Nobody typed in a rule that says "this person likes skate fail videos." Instagram studied huge numbers of examples. What you watched all the way through. What you liked. What you scrolled past in half a second. Then it found patterns. <SourceNeeded />

<Callout kind="think">
Think of a time Instagram's feed guessed right, or got it strangely wrong. What had you watched or liked right before that?
</Callout>
