A <Vocab term="privacy policy" /> is a document where a company says what data it collects and what it does with it. Many people skip them. A 2023 Pew survey found 56% of U.S. adults often click "agree" without reading them.

You don't have to read every word. Look for three answers.

What is collected? Look for "information we collect."

Who gets it? Look for words like "share" and "partners."

How do I delete it? Look for "delete," "your choices," or "your rights."

<Callout kind="tip">
Search the page for "share" or "delete" to jump to the right part.
</Callout>

<Callout kind="fact">
As of 2026, a U.S. law called COPPA covers kids under 13. Many apps need a parent's consent to collect their data.
</Callout>
