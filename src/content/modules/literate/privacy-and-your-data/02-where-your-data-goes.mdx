Your data takes a trip. Follow it through four stops.

Collected. An app gathers data when you use it.

Used. A company may use it to target ads or train AI.

Shared. Some apps share data with partners and advertisers.

Sold. A <Vocab term="data broker" /> is a company that collects information about people and sells it.

<Callout kind="fact">
As of 2026, AI companies handle data differently. Some train on what you share unless you opt out.
</Callout>

<Callout kind="think">
Ask this each time: does the app need that data to work?
</Callout>
