You can take back some control. Start with three kinds of change.

Permissions. A <Vocab term="permission" /> is access you give an app, such as to your camera, contacts, or location. Open your phone's settings and look at each app's permissions. Turn off any the app doesn't need.

Settings. Look for choices about location, ads, and who sees your posts. Pick the most private choice you're comfortable with.

Habits. Delete apps you don't use. Pause before you upload your contacts or photos.

<Callout kind="tip">
As of 2026, many phones offer choices like "while using the app" or "never" for location. Pick the narrowest one that still works.
</Callout>

<Callout kind="warning">
Deleting an app doesn't always delete your account. To remove your data, delete the account first.
</Callout>

<Callout kind="think">
Pick two changes you could make this week. Keep your picks to yourself. On a shared or family device, ask a parent or guardian first.
</Callout>
