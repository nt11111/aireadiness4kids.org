<Recap>
- Apps collect more data than you see.
- Data brokers collect personal data and sell it.
- A privacy policy and your permissions show what an app collects and who gets it.
- Small habits protect you: turn off permissions an app doesn't need, pick private settings, and delete accounts you don't use.
</Recap>
