A clip is going around your grade's group chats. It sounds like Jasper saying he got the answers to Friday's test. He says he'll send them to anyone who asks. Jasper is made up.

The clip came from an account you don't recognize. Nothing from your teacher or school mentions it. Some students forward it to call Jasper a cheater. Others pass it on, hoping to get the answers. Jasper tells you he never said it.
