A school is thinking about AI cameras in the lunch line. The cameras would recognize each student and charge their account, so nobody waits to swipe a card. This school is made up.

<Callout kind="think">
What is your first reaction? Yes, no, or not sure? Keep it to yourself for now.
</Callout>

Here is one side. Lines move faster, and nobody forgets a card.

Here is the other side. The cameras watch every student, every day, and someone has to store those faces.

Both sides have a point. Many AI choices work this way. You gain something and give something up. That is a <Vocab term="trade-off" />: getting more of one thing by giving up some of another.

Thinking about what is right, fair, and good is called <Vocab term="ethics" />. This module gives you a way to think through hard calls, and to explain your choice.
