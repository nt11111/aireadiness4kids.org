<Recap>
- **Bias** is a pattern that unfairly favors or works against some people or groups.
- AI tools learn from **training data**. If the data leaves people out or carries old unfairness, the tool can learn it.
- Bias can enter through **the data**, **the goal people choose**, and **how a tool is tested and used**.
- A **feedback loop** can make bias grow over time.
- People can catch bias by asking what a tool learned from, testing it with many different groups, and letting people question its decisions.
</Recap>

## Questions to carry with you

When you meet a new AI tool, ask:

1. What did it learn from?
2. Who decided what it should do?
3. Who was it tested on, and who could it leave out?
