Take a few minutes to connect this to your own life. There are no wrong answers, and nobody else will see what you write.

<Reflect prompt="Think of an app or tool that makes choices for you, like what to watch, what to buy, or which posts you see. Who might it work well for? Who might it work poorly for? Why?" />

<Callout kind="tip">
Stuck? Picture someone who speaks a different language, lives somewhere else, is much older or younger than you, or uses a screen reader.
</Callout>

<Discuss>
- Share an example, without personal details, of an app that seemed to get someone wrong.
- If you could ask the makers of that app one question about fairness, what would it be?
</Discuss>
