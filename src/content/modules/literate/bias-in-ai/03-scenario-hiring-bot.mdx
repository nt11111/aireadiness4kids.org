scenario:
  prompt: "You're on the program's teen advisory board, and the leader asks what you think. What would you do?"
  options:
    - id: use-it
      text: "Say yes. A computer will be fairer than people, because it doesn't have feelings."
      feedback: "It's true the tool won't play favorites on purpose. But it learned from past summers, when most hires came from one school. It may rank students from other schools lower, not because they'd do a worse job, but because it rarely saw anyone like them get hired. Having no feelings isn't the same as being fair."
    - id: test-first
      text: "Ask what data it learned from, and test it on applications from many different schools before using it."
      feedback: "This is how people catch bias before it hurts anyone. If testing shows the tool ranks some schools lower for no good reason, the board can fix it, add checks, or decide not to use it."
    - id: lottery
      text: "Skip the AI. Pick students with a random lottery instead."
      feedback: "A lottery is easy to explain and gives everyone the same chance, which many people see as fair. The trade-off is that it can't match students to jobs they'd be good at or care about. Choosing not to use AI is a real option, and sometimes the best one."
    - id: explain-appeal
      text: "Use it, but tell students how it works and let anyone who is turned down ask a person for a second look."
      feedback: "Being open about how decisions are made, and letting people appeal, are important protections that can catch some mistakes. But if the tool itself is biased, many students may not know to appeal. Pairing this with testing would be even stronger."
---

A town runs a summer jobs program for teens. This year, far more students applied than there are jobs. To save time, the program leader wants to use an AI tool that ranks the applications.

The tool learned from the applications of teens who were hired in past summers. In those years, most of the hired teens came from one high school, because that's the only school where the program used to advertise.

Before you choose: who might this tool work well for? Who might it work poorly for?
