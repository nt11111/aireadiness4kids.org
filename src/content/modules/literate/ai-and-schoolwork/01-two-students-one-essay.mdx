title: "Two students, one essay"
type: explainer
minutes: 5
---

Meet two students with the same essay assignment. Both open the same AI tool. It's <Vocab term="generative AI" />, which means AI that makes new text, images, or audio from a prompt.

The first student writes a rough draft alone. Then they ask the tool for feedback and fix the weak parts.

The second student pastes in the assignment. They turn in whatever comes back.

<Callout kind="think">
Which student crossed a line? Why is that harder to say than it looks?
</Callout>

Both students used the same tool, and both got help. So the line can't be about the tool. This module is about where it is.
