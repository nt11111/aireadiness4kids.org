AI help runs along a range. Brainstorming and explaining a concept come first. Next is feedback on your draft. Then comes rewriting your sentences. Last is writing the whole thing.

<Callout kind="tip">
Try the explain-it-back test: could I explain this without the AI? If yes, you probably learned. If no, the tool did the thinking.
</Callout>

<Vocab term="plagiarism" /> means presenting someone else's words or ideas as your own. <Vocab term="academic honesty" /> means doing your own work and being open about help. <Vocab term="disclosure" /> means saying when you used a tool.

One reason for AI rules: class is practice, and teachers need to see what you can do. Classes practice different skills, so rules differ. Check the assignment directions or ask your teacher.
