## Timing for a 60-90 minute class

| Segment | 60 min | 90 min | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Pre-check | 5 | 5 | Learners answer the three questions. Keep their answers to compare at the end. |
| Step 1: Hook | 8 | 10 | Read the two students, take a vote on who crossed a line, then ask why it's hard to say. |
| Step 2: Core idea | 15 | 22 | Walk through the range, teach the explain-it-back test, then run the card sort in small groups. |
| Step 3: Scenario | 10 | 18 | Learners vote on A to D, then talk through the follow-up. |
| Step 4: Quick check | 5 | 8 | Answer the four questions, then go over each explanation. |
| Step 5: Reflect | 10 | 17 | Private writing first, then the discussion prompts. |
| Step 6: Recap and post-check | 7 | 10 | Read the recap, repeat the three pre-check questions, and compare. |

## Before you start (sensitive spots, what to avoid)

- AI rules differ by school and by teacher. Do not tell learners what their school allows. Tell them how to find out: the assignment directions, the class handout, or their teacher.
- Don't ask learners to say whether they have used AI on a real assignment. Reflect is private, so invite opinions about rules instead of confessions.
- The three "depends" cards are meant to be debated. Don't settle them with a verdict.
- The scenario and examples are made up. Don't ask learners to sign up for or try any AI tool. Check a tool's age rules before anyone uses it.
- Name AI products only as examples. Don't recommend one.

## Discussion prompts

Use the three in Step 5. For extra time, return to the Step 1 question: which student crossed a line, and why is it hard to say?

## Answer notes

- Step 1: There is no single right answer. Both students used the same tool, so steer toward who did the thinking and the explain-it-back test.
- Step 2 sort: Cards 1, 2, and 4 go in "helps." Cards 5 and 6 go in "skips." Cards 9, 10, and 11 go in "depends." For the "depends" cards, ask what would make each one okay and who decides.
- Step 3: A and D are the strongest choices. B and C cost more. For the follow-up, learners can say they'd still quiz themselves from notes or make flashcards by hand.
- Step 4 answers: C, B, D, A.
- Pre/post check answers: B, D, A.
