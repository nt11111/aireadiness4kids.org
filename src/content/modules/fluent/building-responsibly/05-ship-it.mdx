      text: "Launch with a warning label."
      feedback: "A label puts the problem on users. Students who want answers will ignore it, and the teachers who are harmed never see it."
    - id: d
      text: "Launch to one class, with a report button and a person watching the logs."
      feedback: "Strong. A small launch with monitoring finds problems before scale. It only works if someone acts on what they find."
